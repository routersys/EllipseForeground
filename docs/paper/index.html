EllipseForeground paper

楕円による大まかな指定からの前景抽出
デンドログラム上の最適な切断と手がかりの信頼度に基づく重み付け

The paper is written in Japanese. In English, it is about finding the foreground of an image from a rough ellipse, by an optimal cut of a hierarchical tree whose cues are weighted by how reliable they are. The PDF stays in your browser and nothing is uploaded.
